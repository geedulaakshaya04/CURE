CURE

City Unified Response & Evolution Platform

Anurag University • Department of Artificial Intelligence

The Core Problem

Slide 02 / 20
  • Isolated Grievance Redressal: Modern portals operate in silos. If a water pipe burst destroys a road, the Water Dept and Road Dept repair independently, causing repeat digging.
  • Reactive Operations Trap: Crews are deployed only *after* citizen reports are logged, ignoring preventive maintenance potentials.
  • Siloed Systems: No automated interdependency mapping between complaints, weather metrics, and municipal budgets.

The Reactive Cycle

Citizen Files Ticket ➔ Officer Repairs ➔ Case Closed ➔ Failure Repeats. No systemic root-cause learning is captured.

Systems Thinking Concept

Slide 03 / 20
  • Donella Meadows Framework: CURE models cities as integrated feedback networks of budgets, climate, and infrastructure, not flat tables.
  • Causal Dependencies: Understanding relationships: *High Rainfall ➔ Clogged Sewerage ➔ Flooding ➔ Road Wear ➔ Traffic Gridlock*.
  • Proactive Interventions: Breaking failure loops by shifting budgets from reactive repairs to predictive maintenance schedules.

Feedback Loop Dynamics

Mapping reinforcing feedback loops ($R$) that degrade infrastructure and balancing loops ($B$) that stabilize urban resilience.

Proposed Architecture

Slide 04 / 20
  • Pillar 1: Citizen Intelligence Gateway: Natural speech voice filing, computer vision scanner, and ticket tracking.
  • Pillar 2: Governance Intelligence Engine (GIE): Central GIE AI brain coordinating classification, duplicates, and graphs.
  • Pillar 3: Govt Decision Support System (GDSS): Living Digital Twin map, budget debate engines, and ethics checkers.

Layered Redressal

Unifying citizen gateways with structural digital twins to generate joint multi-agency dispatches.
